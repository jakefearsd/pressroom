## Findings

1. **Revoking a token by SQL does not take effect at once.** Setting `revoked_at` directly in the database left the token authenticating, because the app's JPA cache still held the row. A revoke through the app (UI, or `DELETE /api/v1/tokens/{id}`) goes through JPA and takes effect immediately. The two smoke tokens are revoked in the database and expire on their own within 15 minutes of minting. Use the UI for real revocations.
