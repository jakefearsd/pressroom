# 0.9.1: six follow-ups to the post-page discovery wave

2026-11-12. Approved in chat ("do them all in a single patch release").

## Why

0.9.0 left six small gaps, each seen on production or hit while shipping it.
None needs a new page or a new concept.

## Non-goals

- Category hub pages, table of contents, front-page search box.
- Any change to the share row's contents or the sharing rule in
  `docs/design/design-system.md`.
- A site-config API: `GET/PATCH /v1/admin/config` already exists for an
  admin token (the 404 seen on 2026-11-11 was a blog-pinned token).

## Acceptance criteria

### A. Popular rail counts the headline's blog
1. On the front page, the blog of the lead story counts toward the
   two-per-blog cap of "Popular right now": with the lead from blog X, the
   rail holds at most one more post of X while other blogs can fill it.
2. The cap is still relaxed only when other blogs run out (rail never
   shorter than it would be uncapped).
3. Random picks are unchanged by this work.

### B. Cross-blog Nearby
4. When the post's own blog yields fewer than 3 posts within 80 km, the
   remaining places are filled with other blogs' published posts with
   coordinates within 80 km, nearest first, after the same-blog ones.
5. A cross-blog item shows its blog's name; a same-blog item does not.
6. Only blogs that the front page's "more from the site" would show are
   eligible (same visibility/enabled rule; never a draft, never a
   scheduled post, never an inactive blog).
7. The cross-blog lookup is one query bounded by a latitude/longitude box
   and a row limit; it does not load every located post on the site. It
   does not run when the own blog already filled all 3 places or the post
   has no coordinates.
8. A post shown under Nearby (either kind) is not repeated under Keep
   reading. Keep reading stays same-blog.
9. The cross-blog item's link is that blog's absolute permalink (works
   across virtual hosts and at any context path).

### C. Byline, updated date, breadcrumbs
10. In journal, travel and portfolio, a post page shows the author's
    display name and the publication date near the title (add only what a
    theme does not already show; one of each, never duplicated).
11. When the post was updated on a later calendar day than it was
    published, the page also shows "Updated <date>" in a `<time>` with a
    machine-readable `datetime`; otherwise no updated line.
12. A post page emits one `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD: site front page, the
    blog, the post's category (when the category has a public URL), the
    post. Absolute URLs; names JSON-escaped (a title with a quote or `</`
    cannot break out of the script element).
13. Static pages and list pages are unchanged.

### D. Hot-linked image check
14. The entry SEO checks report a warning when the post body references an
    image on another host that is not this site's media (absolute `http(s)`
    `src` whose host is neither the site's nor the blog's own host). One
    warning per entry, naming the distinct hosts and the count.
15. Relative URLs, `data:` URIs, and this site's media URLs raise nothing.
16. The warning appears wherever the other entry SEO checks appear (editor
    and API), with a message that names the fix (import the image).

### E. Subscribe form status
17. Blog settings shows, next to the newsletter list field, whether the
    reader subscribe form is on or off: off with the reason when no list is
    set (or newsletters are not configured site-wide), on otherwise.
18. The status is text, not colour alone.

### F. Share row switch and translations
19. A per-blog boolean (default on; existing blogs on after migration
    `V046`) turns the share row off. Off: no `nav.entry-share`, no share
    style or script; Nearby and Keep reading are unaffected.
20. The switch is in blog settings and in the API's blog settings, and the
    form/API parity test covers it. The name never uses "share link(s)"
    (that term means the tokened URLs removed in V020).
21. Every reader-facing message key added in 0.9.0 and 0.9.1
    (`afterEntry.*`, byline/updated) has a translation in each of the seven
    locale files; admin-only keys may stay English.
