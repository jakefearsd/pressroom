# Pressroom 0.18.0: reader and admin experience

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-reader-and-admin-experience-design.md`.

## For readers

- **Blogs on a custom domain show no reader controls** (no Save, Follow, Read
  marks or rows). The sign-in cookie is not carried to another domain, so the
  page cannot know the reader.
- **Sign-in is the provider's button** (Google in production) in the blog
  header, under the comments, on the sign-in page and wherever a reader wants
  something. Authors sign in from the colophon's Sign in, one click from the
  password form.
- **Save** an article (in the share row) and **Follow** a blog (in the
  blog's masthead and at the end of each article). Signed out, the click
  becomes the sign-in, and the save or follow completes once on return.
- **Reading history** on every device: an article counts as read after ten
  seconds on the page. Blog homes and the front page show **Continue
  reading** and **From blogs you follow**, list items the reader has read
  carry a small **Read** label, and **Places you've been** comes from the
  history while signed in.
- After following a blog with a newsletter, **"Also email me new posts"**
  subscribes the account's email to that blog's listmonk list (listmonk sends
  its own confirmation and owns the unsubscribe).
- A **"You" menu** in the header (initials and name) leads to the account
  page: Saved, Following, Reading history (with "Clear history" and "Keep my
  reading history"), Your comments ("Answered by {author}" when the author
  replied), Email, Display name and Delete account.

## Reply emails and unsubscribing

- When a blog's author replies to a reader's comment, the reader gets one
  email: subject "{author} replied to your comment on {entry title}", the
  comment and the reply as plain text, and a link to the comment.
- It is on by default and only goes to an account with an email address,
  when mail is configured, and never for a reply to one's own comment.
- Every email carries an unsubscribe link. It needs no sign-in: one button
  turns reply emails off, and the account page's Email switch turns them back
  on. Over https the email also carries `List-Unsubscribe` and
  `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, so mail clients offer
  their own one-click unsubscribe.

## For authors

- The admin home shows what needs you; the sidebar carries counts; one
  approval queue covers every blog; the entries list puts results above the
  fold; the editor's publish panel stays on screen.

## Privacy page wording (for the operator to paste into the site's privacy page)

The production privacy page is a site page the operator edits; this release
does not change it. Suggested paragraph:

> **If you sign in.** When you sign in to comment, save or follow, we keep the
> email address and name your sign-in provider gives us, the articles you
> save, the blogs you follow, and a history of the articles you have read (an
> article counts as read after you have spent ten seconds on it). We use them
> only to show them back to you: on your account page, in "Continue reading",
> "From blogs you follow" and "Places you've been", and in the small "Read"
> marks on lists. You can remove any save or follow you can see on your
> account page, clear your reading history, or turn the history off ("Keep
> my reading history"), at any time. A save or follow of a post or blog that
> is no longer public is kept but not shown; "Clear history" and deleting
> your account remove everything. When an author replies to one of your comments we
> send you one email about it; every such email has a link to stop them, and
> the switch is on your account page too. If you choose "Email me new posts"
> for a blog, your email address is passed to that blog's newsletter service,
> which asks you to confirm and lets you unsubscribe from every message.
> Deleting your account removes your comments, saves, follows and reading
> history. We do not sell or share any of this.

## Upgrade notes

- Migrations V055 (admin "new" state), V056 (reader state tables) and V057
  (reader preference columns) apply on start or with `./pressroom migrate`.
- The reply-email unsubscribe key (`mail.unsubscribe.secret`) is generated on
  first start if absent.
- Reader features appear only with reader sign-in (SSO) configured; without
  it the blogs render as in 0.17.1.
